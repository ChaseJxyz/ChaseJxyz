### Hello!

I'm Chase J!(.xyz). I consider myself a "digital storyteller," as I love creating stories in and for digital formats. That usually means writing words to be read on the web, and sometimes interactive stuff.

I hold a Bachelor of Science degree in journalism with a creative writing minor (and took some web design/management classes as part of that) from the Rochester Institute of Technology (go tigers 🐯). I spent too much time in/around the game design & development department, and it shows.

I'm currently working on a second Bachelor's degree for computer science with an information security concentration from Southern New Hampshire University. I recently finished SANS Technology Institute's Cyber Academy immersion program, so I'm [GIAC certified](https://www.giac.org/certified-professional/Chase-Anderson/251906), also the Sec+.

The day job is marketplace integration management and digital marketing analysis/management at a B2B \+ B2C book retailer, plus about a dozen other hats as required. My favorite parts are when I get to do incident handling, forensics, and searching for fraud (aka when I get to do security stuff). I'm always looking for opportunities to do more!
